    The surprise that is not your fault

    On September 5 you went through your lit gold-crowned gate 132, gathered crimson stems, and came back out near gate 120, 563 blocks from where you started. You did nothing wrong. When you step through, the game looks for any portal already within about 128 blocks of the matching Nether spot and sends you there. Both of your gates land on the same Nether portal: on the map, their two lines meet at one point.

    A plain compass spins in the Nether, so your real compass there is your Position readout. You already read it on every trip. That habit is what gets you home.

    4

    When you feel lost: stop, read, walk

    1. Stop and wall in. Place cobblestone around you, two high, to make a sheltered spot. Breathe.
    2. Read your Position. Type it into "I'm lost" at the top of this card.
    3. Walk the difference. The card tells you which way and how far, not a safe path: watch for lava and drops. Small numbers in the Nether are short walks.
    4. Follow your torches. Torches on your right going out are on your left coming home.

    5

    Three big moments, three calm answers

    A ghast

    Loud, but slow. Raise your shield, or step behind a cobblestone block. Ghasts drift over open lava seas and are rare in the crimson forest you know. If one breaks your portal, flint and steel lights it again.

    Lava

    Drink fire resistance before you get close. Then lay cobblestone one block at a time and walk slowly. Your potion lasts minutes; your bridge lasts forever.

    Piglins and hoglins

    With gold boots on, piglins are calm. Don't open chests or break gold near them. Hoglins, the big pink boars of the crimson forest, ignore gold: give them room, or stand two blocks up and let them wander off.
